COOKIES AND DEVICE STORAGE

Effective date: 15 October 2026 · Last reviewed: 15 October 2026

This notice supplements the Privacy Policy. Websites and web apps can store information on or access information from your device using cookies, local storage, service workers and similar technologies.

What may be used

  • Essential session and security storage: to support sign-in, maintain a session, protect accounts and provide core functions requested by you.
  • App preferences: to remember settings or installation-related state where the app uses those features.
  • Progressive web app and service-worker storage: to support installation, app updates and push notifications when enabled.
  • Third-party technologies: authentication, hosting, security or embedded services may use their own storage or access technologies.

Consent and control

Strictly necessary technologies may be used where the law permits an exemption. Non-essential analytics, advertising or other storage/access technologies must not be used without consent where consent is required by applicable law. We do not intend this notice to imply that any particular analytics or advertising technology is currently active; the live production build and its third-party scripts must be audited to confirm the exact inventory.

You can manage some storage through your browser or device settings. Blocking essential storage may prevent sign-in, calls, notifications or other app functions from working. Removing site data may sign you out, remove local preferences or disable the installed app until it refreshes.

Changes

We will update this notice if the technologies used by GAYZE change materially. Before introducing non-essential technologies that require consent, we must provide clear information and a valid consent mechanism.
