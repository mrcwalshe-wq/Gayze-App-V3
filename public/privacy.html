PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: 15 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GAYZE (“we”, “us” or “our”), operating www.gayze.co.uk (the “Service”), collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use the Service. It should be read with our Terms and Conditions.

1. Introduction

We use information to provide, maintain, secure and improve GAYZE. Where applicable law requires consent or another lawful basis, we will rely on the appropriate basis for the relevant processing.

2. Definitions

Personal Data means information relating to an identified or identifiable living person. Usage Data means technical and interaction information generated when the Service is used. Cookies are small files stored on your device. Data Controller means the organisation that determines why and how Personal Data is processed. Data Processors process data on the Controller’s behalf. User means the person using the Service.

3. Information Collection and Use

We collect information needed to operate the Service, support accounts and communications, provide discovery and safety features, maintain security, troubleshoot faults and improve functionality.

4. Types of Data Collected

Account and Personal Data: information you provide may include email address, name, phone number, profile details and other information you choose to submit.

Usage and Device Data: this may include IP address, browser and device type, operating system, pages or features used, timestamps, session duration, device identifiers and diagnostic information.

Location Data: if you grant permission, we may process location information to centre the map and provide location-based discovery and related features. You can manage device location permissions in your device settings. GAYZE may use privacy-protective location handling; consult in-app controls for available options.

Cookies and similar technologies: these may support sessions, preferences, security, analytics and other functions. You can manage cookies through your browser; disabling them may affect functionality.

Other information: we may process profile, preference, communication, safety and account information that you choose to provide or that is generated through your use of the Service. We do not ask you to provide sensitive identifiers unless specifically required and explained for a legitimate purpose.

5. Use of Data

We may use information to provide and maintain the Service; manage accounts and subscriptions; enable interactive features and communications; provide support; analyse usage and improve features; monitor and address security, fraud and technical issues; comply with legal obligations; enforce agreements; and carry out purposes explained when information is collected or otherwise permitted by law.

We will send marketing communications where permitted by law and provide a way to opt out.

6. Retention of Data

We retain Personal Data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, including legal, accounting, security and dispute-resolution requirements. Retention periods vary by data type and purpose.

7. International Transfers

Information may be processed in the United Kingdom or other countries where we or our service providers operate. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

8. Disclosure of Data

We may share information with service providers supporting hosting, authentication, communications, analytics, payments and security; with professional advisers; as part of a merger, acquisition or asset transfer; where required by law or valid legal process; to protect rights, safety and security; or with your direction or consent. We do not disclose information for unrelated purposes without a lawful basis.

9. Security of Data

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

10. Your Data Protection Rights (UK GDPR and GDPR)

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict processing of your Personal Data, object to processing, receive certain data in a portable format, and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also complain to the relevant data protection authority. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

To make a privacy request, email gayze.co.uk@gmail.com.

11. California Online Privacy (CalOPPA)

This policy is publicly available and we will update it when material changes are made. Contact us using the email below to request access to or correction of account information. Browser “Do Not Track” signals may not have a uniform technical standard; our handling will be as described in this policy and applicable law.

12. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents may have rights to know, access, correct or delete certain personal information, obtain information about disclosures, opt out of certain sales or sharing, and not be unlawfully discriminated against for exercising their rights. To submit a request, email gayze.co.uk@gmail.com. We will respond as required by applicable law.

13. Service Providers

We may use third-party companies and individuals to host, operate, support, secure and analyse the Service. They may process Personal Data on our behalf subject to appropriate obligations and instructions.

14. Analytics

We may use analytics and diagnostic services to understand how the Service is used and to improve reliability and usability.

15. Development and Deployment Tools

We may use third-party development, testing, deployment and monitoring services to maintain the Service.

16. Advertising and Remarketing

If advertising or remarketing technologies are used, their operation and any required consent or opt-out choices will be provided in accordance with applicable law. You can contact us with questions about these technologies.

17. Payments

If paid features are offered, payment details may be handled directly by third-party payment processors under their own privacy policies. We do not intend to store full payment card details ourselves.

18. Links to Other Sites

The Service may link to third-party websites. We do not control their content or privacy practices and encourage you to read their policies.

19. Children’s Privacy

GAYZE is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with Personal Data, contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

20. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice.
