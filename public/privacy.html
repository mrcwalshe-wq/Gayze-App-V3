PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: 27 October 2026

This policy explains how GAYZE collects and uses personal information when you use the GAYZE website and mobile-friendly web app, including when you install it on your device’s Home Screen. Please read it with our Terms and Conditions.

1. Who is responsible for your data?

The data controller is Gayze Labs Ltd, a company established in the United Kingdom (“GAYZE”, “we”, “us” or “our”). For privacy questions or requests, contact gayze.co.uk@gmail.com.

GAYZE is designed primarily for users in the United Kingdom. We apply the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018 and other applicable UK laws. Other laws may also apply depending on where you live and how the Service is offered to you. This policy does not remove any rights you have under mandatory local law.

2. What GAYZE does

GAYZE is an adult, location-based social discovery service. Its features may include user profiles and profile photos; a map and proximity-based discovery; time-limited availability or intent signals; Social intent signals; sending a “Gayze” to another eligible user; connections and chat; live audio and video calls; push notifications; and tools to block or report users.

Right Now and Later signals are time-limited (currently designed around a short, approximately two-hour Right Now window and a longer, approximately 24-hour Later window). The app’s controls and actual behaviour determine which features are available to you at a given time. A signal or map result is not a guarantee that another user is available, nearby, genuine or safe.

3. Information we process

  • Account and sign-in information: account identifiers, email address and information returned by your sign-in provider, such as Google if you choose Google sign-in.
  • Profile information: display name, profile photos, age or other profile fields you provide, preferences and information you choose to share.
  • Location and discovery information: device location when you grant permission, map position, selected search radius, and approximate or privacy-protected intent locations used to show relevant discovery results. Your exact GPS position is not intended to be displayed to other users; users may see an approximate intent location instead.
  • Intent and interaction information: whether you select Social or Private/Intimate activity, your Right Now or Later availability, activity preferences, Gayzes sent or received, eligibility and rate-limit records, connections, and actions taken in the app.
  • Messages and calls: chat messages, information or media you choose to send, delivery/read or conversation events where implemented, and call-related information such as participants, timestamps, connection/signalling data and call status. Live calls require access to your microphone and, for video, your camera after you grant permission and participate in a call.
  • Safety information: block and report records, the information included in a report, and records needed to investigate abuse, enforce our rules or protect users.
  • Notifications: push subscription details, device/browser endpoint information and notification preferences needed to deliver app notifications, where you enable them.
  • Technical and diagnostic information: IP address and request logs, browser/device and operating-system details, timestamps, error and performance information, and data needed to maintain account security and the Service.

Please do not include sensitive information in your profile or messages unless you choose to share it. Because GAYZE is a social meeting app, your use of it, your profile and your preferences may reveal or allow others to infer information about your sexual orientation. Where this constitutes special-category personal data under UK GDPR, we will only process it where a valid Article 9 condition applies; where we rely on consent, that consent must be explicit and you can withdraw it for future processing.

4. Location and map privacy

GAYZE requests location permission after sign-in so it can centre your map and provide proximity discovery. If you deny or withdraw permission, location-dependent features may not work as intended. You can change permission in your device or browser settings.

Location is used to calculate relevant discovery results and show approximate or privacy-protected intent locations. The app’s distance controls are designed to limit discovery to the radius you select, up to a maximum of 5 km. Location can still reveal sensitive information, especially when combined with repeated activity or other profile details. Do not assume that an approximate map marker makes you impossible to identify.

5. How we use information and our lawful bases

We use information to create and secure accounts; display profiles and eligible discovery results; publish and expire intent signals; enforce category and Gayze limits; provide chat, calls and notifications; operate blocking and reporting; respond to support requests; diagnose faults; prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents; comply with law; and maintain and improve the Service.

Depending on the purpose, our lawful basis may be: performing our contract with you; our legitimate interests in operating, securing and improving GAYZE, balanced against your rights; compliance with a legal obligation; or your consent. We will seek consent where required, including for device permissions and any special-category processing that relies on consent. You can withdraw consent through the relevant device/app controls or by contacting us, although this does not make earlier lawful processing unlawful.

6. Messages, audio/video calls and notifications

Your messages and content are processed to deliver conversations and provide the communication features you use. Live audio/video calls use real-time communication technology and may require connection signalling and network information to establish a call. We do not describe calls or messages as end-to-end encrypted unless the relevant feature’s implementation has been verified to provide that protection. Do not send information through GAYZE that you would not want the intended recipient to receive or retain.

Push notifications depend on your browser/device permissions and notification subscription. Turning off notifications in your device or browser settings can stop them. Notifications may expose message or call information on your lock screen depending on your device settings.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own direct marketing unless we explain this and have a lawful basis.

8. International transfers

Some service providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where UK data protection law requires safeguards for an international transfer, we will use an applicable safeguard, such as UK adequacy regulations or an approved contractual mechanism, and take additional steps where required.

9. Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including account operation, safety, security, legal obligations and dispute handling. Intent signals are designed to expire after their relevant time window, but associated records may be retained for operational, safety or security purposes. Messages, account records, reports and technical logs may remain for different periods. Deleting an account or content may not immediately remove information from backups or records we must retain by law or for legitimate safety and legal purposes.

We do not currently publish a fixed retention period for every data type. You can request information about retention by emailing us.
