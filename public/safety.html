SAFETY AND REPORTING

Effective date: 15 October 2026 · Last reviewed: 15 October 2026

Your safety matters, but GAYZE cannot verify every user or guarantee that an interaction or meeting will be safe. Use your judgement and do not share information you are uncomfortable disclosing.

Report a user or content

  1. Where an in-app report control is available, open the relevant profile or conversation and submit a report.
  2. Include the username or profile link, what happened, approximate date/time and relevant context. Only include screenshots or other evidence you can share safely and lawfully.
  3. If you cannot report in the app, email gayze.co.uk@gmail.com with “GAYZE safety report” in the subject. Avoid including unnecessary sensitive information in the first email.
  4. If you need to stop contact, use the in-app block feature where available and do not engage further.

What happens next?

We review reports as resources and circumstances allow. We may request more information, restrict content or accounts, preserve relevant records, or refer matters to authorities where appropriate and lawful. We cannot promise a specific response time or outcome. Do not use reports to harass or knowingly make false accusations against another user.

Immediate danger or crime

If you or another person is in immediate danger in the UK, call 999. For non-emergency police matters, call 101. Preserve evidence if safe, tell someone you trust and consider meeting only in public places. GAYZE is not an emergency response service.

Safer meetings

  • Meet in a public place and arrange your own transport.
  • Tell a trusted person where you are going and agree a check-in time.
  • Keep control of your drink, phone and belongings.
  • Do not assume profile details or intent markers have been independently verified.
  • Leave and seek help if you feel pressured or unsafe.

Blocking and privacy

Blocking is intended to limit further interaction, but it may not erase information another user already saw, saved or captured. For personal-data requests, use the instructions on the Account and Data Deletion page or read the Privacy Policy.

Contact

Safety reports: gayze.co.uk@gmail.com.
