ACCOUNT AND DATA DELETION

Effective date: 15 October 2026 · Last reviewed: 15 October 2026

You can request closure of your GAYZE account and deletion of associated personal data by emailing gayze.co.uk@gmail.com with the subject GAYZE account deletion.

Include

  • The email address or sign-in method associated with your account.
  • Your GAYZE username or profile identifier, if known.
  • A clear statement that you want your account deleted.

Do not send your password or identity documents unless we specifically request proportionate verification through a secure method. We may ask for limited information to verify that the request comes from the account holder.

What happens to the request?

We will review the request, take reasonable steps to close or disable the account and erase or anonymise personal data where required and technically possible. Some information may need to be retained for a limited period to comply with law, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, protect users, or establish or defend legal claims. Any retained data remains subject to appropriate access controls and will not be kept longer than necessary for the relevant purpose.

Deletion from active systems may not immediately remove copies in protected backups or records already received by other users. Messages or content that another person has lawfully saved may remain in their possession. We will explain any applicable exception where required by law.

Response and rights

We aim to respond without undue delay and within the time limits that apply under data protection law. We may need to verify your identity. You can also request access, correction, restriction or other applicable data rights using the same contact address. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Important: This page provides a manual email request route. It does not mean an automated in-app account-deletion workflow has been implemented or tested.
