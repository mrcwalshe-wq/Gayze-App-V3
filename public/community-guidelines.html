COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

Effective date: 15 October 2026 · Last reviewed: 27 October 2026

GAYZE is intended for adults. These guidelines apply to profiles, photos, location and intent signals, Gayzes, messages, calls and all other interactions. Violations may lead to content removal, restrictions or account suspension.

Respect consent

  • A match, Gayze, profile view or intent signal is not consent to sexual activity or further contact.
  • Respect “no”, a lack of response, blocks and requests to stop. Do not repeatedly contact someone after they decline.
  • Do not send unsolicited sexual images, explicit content or sexual messages.
  • Never share intimate images, private messages or identifying details without permission, except where a lawful report to protect someone requires it.

Keep content suitable for a social app

  • GAYZE is a social meeting app for adults. Profiles, photos, bios, intents and chat names must not contain nudity, sexually explicit images or text, or sexual solicitation.
  • Do not use GAYZE to advertise or arrange paid sexual services.
  • We may remove content or restrict accounts that break these rules without notice.

No harassment or abuse

  • No threats, stalking, coercion, blackmail, hate speech, targeted abuse or discriminatory harassment.
  • No doxxing, threats to out someone, publishing a person’s location, or attempting to identify someone from approximate markers.
  • No impersonation, catfishing for fraud, scams, extortion or deceptive requests for money.
  • No exploitation, trafficking, non-consensual sexual content or any sexual content involving anyone under 18.

Protect privacy and safety

  • Do not share another person’s contact details, precise location, photos or personal information without a lawful basis and permission where required.
  • Do not use automated tools, scraping, surveillance or repeated accounts to track users or evade limits.
  • Do not attempt to bypass category eligibility, Gayze rate limits, blocks, account restrictions or security controls.
  • Keep your own profile accurate and only upload photos and content you have the right to use.

Use intents honestly

Choose your intent honestly and respect the intent preferences shown by other users. Do not send a Gayze or continue an interaction where the Service indicates the recipient is not eligible. A change in another user’s intent does not grant consent to anything beyond the interaction they choose to accept.

Reporting and enforcement

Use in-app report and block tools where available, or follow the steps on our Safety and Reporting page. Include usernames, approximate times and relevant screenshots where safe and lawful. Do not retaliate against someone for reporting. We may investigate, remove content, restrict accounts or contact appropriate authorities where necessary and lawful. We cannot guarantee a particular outcome or response time.

Emergencies

If someone is in immediate danger in the UK, call 999. For non-emergency police matters, call 101. Do not rely on GAYZE support as an emergency service.
