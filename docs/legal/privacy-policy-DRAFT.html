7. Location

We ask for location permission so we can centre your map and find people within the radius you choose (up to 5 km). Other members see an approximate position, not where you actually are. Even so, repeated activity or other details could allow someone to work out where you are, so please take care. You can turn location off in your device settings; some features will then stop working.

8. Messages and calls

Messages are end-to-end encrypted. They are encrypted on your device with keys that are held on your devices, and our servers store only the encrypted text and the details needed to deliver it (who, when and how large). We cannot read your messages. Because of this, if you lose the device that holds your keys, or sign in on a new device that has not been given them, older messages cannot be recovered; we cannot restore them. If you report a message we can see only what is included in the report [TO CONFIRM: describe exactly what a report captures].

Calls are sent between devices using standard real-time technology and are encrypted in transit; relay servers provided by Cloudflare may carry the call when a direct connection is not possible [TO CONFIRM: confirm wording with the engineer who built calls].

Push notifications are delivered through your browser or device maker’s push service (Apple, Google or Mozilla). [TO CONFIRM: state what notification text can appear on a lock screen]
